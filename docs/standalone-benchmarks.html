Methodology

LLM: MiniCPM5-1B or Phi-4-mini int4, one turn, temperature 0.8, top-k 40, top-p 0.95, 512-token cap (the ONNX GenAI engine allows up to 1536 total when EOS does not fire). Vector search: MiniLM-L6-v2, 384-d, 20-chunk corpus. Decision: Julia-1, 18-prompt suite.

Energy is an estimate: iOS uses the ~5% gauge or Power Profiler; Android integrates current × voltage at 1 Hz. Compare lanes on the same device, not across devices.

Notes & quirks
  • iOS Core ML runs the decision leaf is reserved — no Julia-1 package exists yet.
  • The LiteRT decision GPU delegate fails to compile on the 15 Pro; on the TCL it compiles but is 7.6× slower than ONNX CPU.
  • Rows tagged 60-run or 2026-10-27 are sustained battery runs, not cold single runs.
